Who is responsible

Controller: DNK Capitals, which owns the trade name Verifika Pro and is established outside the European Union. Contact: .

Representative in the European Union (GDPR Article 27): , , .

What we collect

What your account needs (name, e-mail, language, sign-in and security records). For companies: the company details, the needs, the feedback given and the people of the team. For professionals: the profile, CV, skills, languages, availability, expected rate or salary, interview notes or transcript, verification results and documents you add. Once a mission is signed: the contract and its signature record (name typed, date, time, browser, fingerprint of the document), the timesheets and, for consultants who invoice, the billing details (legal name, company number, VAT number checked with the EU VIES service, address, bank account). E-mails exchanged with our team are kept in the history of your file. We never record date of birth, nationality, photo, identity document numbers or health data.

Where your data comes from

From you, when you create an account, apply or answer us. From a recruiter working with us, who talked with you about a position and sent us your details with your agreement. From the previous client and candidate database of Select Links (our partner, which uses the same team): we write to these people before using their data, and a candidate profile is shown to companies only after the person agrees. From public registers, for the checks of companies and VAT numbers.

Why, and on which basis

Running your account, your missions, the contracts, the signatures and the invoices (contract, and steps before a contract at your request). Presenting a professional to a company: only with the person’s agreement for that position (consent). Keeping a profile for future opportunities and using it to improve our tools: consent, which you can withdraw at any time. Writing a first time to a professional or a company contact about our services, securing the platform and keeping the history of our exchanges: our legitimate interest, which you can object to. Keeping invoices and accounting records: legal obligation.

Artificial intelligence

We use an AI service (Claude, by Anthropic) to help our team: read a CV or a need, point out what a person should check, prepare a draft of an interview report from the notes, draft the presentation of a profile, translate a text, and suggest a reading of a written answer in our recruiter assessment. Before a text is sent, names, e-mail addresses, phone numbers and links are removed. A person reads every result and takes every decision: the AI never decides, never gives a score, never ranks people and never analyses voice, face or emotions. Anthropic does not use these data to train its models. As AI used in recruitment is a high-risk use under the European AI Act, we keep a record of each AI result, of the person who checked it and of what was changed.

Who sees your data

Our team, each person according to their role. Companies see anonymised profiles, then a first name for an interview, then the full identity at signature, and only for a position you said yes to. An agency may pass an anonymised profile to its end client for that position. Recruiters working with us see only the people they brought and the needs they work on, never the prices agreed with companies.

Providers acting on our behalf: OVHcloud (hosting of the portal and of the encrypted backups, in France), Microsoft 365 (e-mails, calendar, Teams meetings and transcripts; Microsoft Clarity on this site with your consent), Anthropic (AI), Google (Google Analytics and Search Console on this site with your consent; no data of the portal), Cloudflare (Turnstile, the check that a form is sent by a person and not a robot), GitHub (hosting of this public site) and Slack (sharing of positions inside the team, without candidate data). Some of them are outside the European Union; transfers rely on the European Commission’s adequacy decision for the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or on standard contractual clauses.

Measurement of the site and the portal

On this public site, with your consent only: Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity measure pages visited, buttons used and scroll, never what you type (form fields are masked). They are never used in the portal. In the portal, we measure screens opened, time of active use and buttons used, never what you type; for clients, candidates and independent recruiters, these measures are pseudonymous and only read in groups of at least 5 people. For Verifika employees, presence in the portal is visible to the administrator and kept 90 days.

When you create an account or send a request, we keep how you reached us (campaign link, previous site), never a personal identifier.

Security

The portal is hosted in France. Data travel encrypted, backups are encrypted every night and a restore is tested regularly. Sign-in uses single-use codes and, for our team, a second factor; sessions expire; each person only reaches what their role needs; every action is recorded in a journal that cannot be changed without it showing. Public forms are protected against robots, and the collection of our pages to train AI models is not allowed. We manage security following the ISO/IEC 27001 standard (we are not certified). To report a security issue, see verifika.pro/.well-known/security.txt.

How long

As long as the account is active, then 24 months after the last contact, then deletion or a request to renew. A person who did not agree to be contacted again is kept only on a do-not-contact list (e-mail address), so that we never write again. Contracts and their signature records: 10 years after the end of the contract. Invoices and accounting records: 10 years (legal obligation). Audience measurement of the site: 13 months. Security records: 12 months.

Recruiting recruiters

When you apply to work as a recruiter with Verifika Pro:

What we collect. Your contact details, your CV, your answers to our assessment, the review of your answers, notes from your interview and our decision. If you join us: your training progress, your certification, the quality checks on your work and what is needed to pay you.

Why, and on what basis. To assess your application and decide whether to work with you: steps before a contract, at your request. To keep your application for future needs: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time. To avoid re-contacting a person whose collaboration ended for a serious breach: our legitimate interest; we keep only the minimum, described below.

How we assess. A person reviews every answer and takes every decision. The AI service may suggest a reading of your written answers against our expected answers; it receives your answers only, without your name or contact details, and never decides.

How long. If you are not retained: 12 months after the decision, then deletion. If you agreed to stay in our pool: 24 months. If you work with us: for the duration of the collaboration and the period required by accounting and tax law after it. After a serious breach: name, hashed contact details, date and a factual reason, for 3 years.

Who sees it. Only the Verifika recruitment team, and our providers listed above on our instructions.

Where. Your data are hosted in France and may be read by our team in the European Union, in Kenya and in Rwanda. Outside the EU, we use the safeguards required by law, such as the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses. If you are in Kenya, you may also complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.

Your rights

Access, correction, deletion, portability, objection, limitation and withdrawal of consent, from your account (download of your data, request to delete) or by e-mail to the contact above. We answer within one month. No decision about you is taken by a machine alone. You may complain to the Belgian Data Protection Authority (dataprotectionauthority.be) or to the authority of your country.
