Who is responsible

Controller: DNK Capitals, which owns the trade name Verifika Pro and is established outside the European Union. Contact: .

Representative in the European Union (GDPR Article 27): , , .

What we collect

What your account needs (name, e-mail, language); for companies, the company details and its needs; for professionals, the profile, CV, skills, languages, availability, expected rate or salary, interview notes and verification results. We never record date of birth, nationality, photo, identity document numbers or health data.

Why, and on which basis

Running your account (contract); verifying and presenting professionals with their agreement for each position (consent and contract); securing the platform and measuring the use of the site (legitimate interest, or your consent for the audience measurement cookies); meeting legal duties (invoices).

Artificial intelligence

AI helps read CVs and needs and points out what a person should check. It never decides, never ranks people and never analyses voice, face or emotions.

Who sees your data

Our team; companies see anonymised profiles, then a first name for an interview, then the full identity at signature, and only for a position you said yes to.

Processors acting on our behalf: Google (portal, files, Google Analytics), Microsoft (e-mails, Teams, Microsoft Clarity), Anthropic (AI), GitHub (hosting of this site). Some of them are outside the European Union; transfers rely on the European Commission's adequacy decision for the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or on standard contractual clauses.

Measurement of the site and the portal

On this public site, with your consent only: Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity measure pages visited, buttons used and scroll, never what you type (form fields are masked). They are never used on the pages of the portal where you are signed in. In the portal, we measure screens opened, time of active use and buttons used, never what you type; for clients, candidates and independent recruiters, these measures are pseudonymous and only read in groups of at least 5 people. For Verifika employees, presence in the portal is visible to the administrator and kept 90 days.

When you create an account or send a request, we keep how you reached us (campaign link, previous site), never a personal identifier.

How long

As long as the account is active, then 24 months after the last contact, except what the law requires us to keep (invoices: 7 years). Audience measurement: 13 months.

Your rights

Access, correction, deletion, portability, objection and withdrawal of consent, from your account or by e-mail. You may complain to the Belgian Data Protection Authority (dataprotectionauthority.be) or to the authority of your country.
