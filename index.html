Questions

What people ask us

How much does it cost?

Nothing for professionals. For companies, publishing a need is free; you pay when a mission starts or a hire is made.

How fast do I receive profiles?

First profiles within 2 working days for the roles we cover. Your feedback on each profile makes the next ones fit better.

I am a freelancer in Africa. Can I work for European companies?

Yes, for remote roles that match your time zone and languages. Verification is what makes the difference.

How long does verification take?

Profiles that match open positions are interviewed first; others as soon as a recruiter of the field is available.

I am an agency. Will you contact my client?

Never. Your end client stays confidential, and a professional is presented to it only once, through you.

How is my data protected?

Single-use sign-in codes, access by role, every action logged. Download your data or ask for deletion at any time.
