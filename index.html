How it works

From the need to the signature, every step is traced.

  1. You publish a need

    Position, mandatory criteria, budget. Our team reviews it with you and may propose a clearer version.

  2. Specialised recruiters search

    Our network of recruiters finds profiles and runs a structured interview, with a full report criterion by criterion.

  3. The Verifika team validates

    A second qualification: identity, confirmed exchange, languages. The verification level reached is shown.

  4. You receive anonymised profiles

    With their evidence. The professional agreed to be presented for this exact position. Your feedback is expected on each profile.

  5. Interview

    You request an interview and accept the non-solicitation clause. The first name is shared for the call.

  6. Signature and follow-up

    The full identity is shared at signature. We follow the mission or the guarantee period with you.

Verification levels

A hallmark, not a score.

Like the hallmark of an assay office on precious metal, each badge says what was checked and when. It is never a score or a ranking.

Level 0 · Declared

Information given by the professional or their CV.

Level 1 · Recruiter interview

Pre-qualification by a specialised recruiter, full report.

Level 2 · Validated by Verifika

Second qualification by the Verifika team: identity, confirmed exchange, language.

Level 3 · Documents verified

Diplomas, certifications or licences checked at the source.

Frequently asked questions

What people often ask us

How much does Verifika Pro cost?

For professionals, nothing. For companies, publishing a need comes with no commitment: you pay when a mission starts (freelance) or a hire is made (permanent), under the terms agreed for each need.

What exactly do you check?

Depending on the level: a structured interview with a report criterion by criterion, a second qualification by our team (identity, exchange, languages), then diplomas or certifications checked at the source. The level and the date of the last check are shown.

Why are profiles anonymised?

To protect professionals and keep the choice on skills. The first name is given for the interview, the full identity at signature.

I am an agency or an independent recruiter: can I use Verifika Pro?

Yes. You name the end client of each need; we keep it confidential and present a professional only once per end client.

How is my data protected?

Sign-in with single-use codes, access limited by role, every action logged, data kept for a limited time. You can download your data or ask for deletion at any time.
